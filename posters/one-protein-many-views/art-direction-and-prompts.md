## Interpretation and limitations

All views are schematic illustrations, not coordinate-based renderings. Repeated shapes aim to maintain a common orientation but do not establish exact atomic registration. Electrostatic and hydrophobicity maps are expressly illustrative, not computed predictions; their separate legends must not be interchanged with atom-element colors. Surface depicts a conceptual molecular boundary rather than a numerically calculated solvent-excluded surface. Pocket illustrations combine binding and catalytic features, which occupy the same active-site cavity. His64 is a proton shuttle, not a zinc ligand. CO2 is positioned near zinc-bound hydroxide and is not drawn as a direct zinc ligand. Hydrogen atoms and complete backbone structure are omitted in the atomic schematic, and water-network contacts are conceptual. Traditional carbonic anhydrase residue numbering is used.
