## Art direction

A rightward-moving replication fork organizes the landscape. Purple parental strands separate at CMG; continuous leading-strand DNA extends upper left, while discontinuous lagging-strand synthesis follows a lower curve. New DNA is cyan and initiating primers are gold. Distinct protein silhouettes identify Pol epsilon, Pol delta, Pol alpha-primase, PCNA, RPA and human accessory factors. The lower strip relates the molecular machine to proteomics, structural reconstruction and simulation.

## Scientific scope

This is a functional schematic combining human core-replisome structures with biochemical evidence. It is not a single experimentally resolved complete assembly, and protein arrangement, stoichiometry and DNA routing are simplified. CMG surrounds the leading template; Pol epsilon performs bulk leading synthesis; Pol alpha-primase initiates RNA-DNA primers and Pol delta extends lagging-strand fragments. Both nascent strands are synthesized 5-prime to 3-prime. PCNA is depicted around nascent duplexes on both branches. The curved lagging template is a drawing convention, not an assertion of an obligatory bacterial-style trombone-loop architecture. The AND-1 depiction does not establish a required tether for priming. The primer-maturation inset summarizes processing and ligation and omits participating enzymes and detailed intermediates. Other omitted processes include full clamp loading, proofreading, chromatin handling, origin activation, repair and checkpoints. Plots, maps and molecular icons are illustrative. Raster concept artwork, not an atomic model or print-ready vector master.
