## Schematic limitations

Illustrated composite of processing events, not an atomic model or a single experimental state. Single cap shown; cap stoichiometry varies. Constitutive human catalytic core. Colors identify functional groups, not measured chemistry. Ubiquitin topology and substrate geometry are simplified; not all ubiquitination targets degradation. Peptides are primary proteasome products; amino-acid recycling requires downstream peptidases. Footer data are illustrative.
