## Scientific scope
The hero depicts two complete mature receptor chains, each with four extracellular domains, one transmembrane helix, a juxtamembrane segment, a bilobed kinase and a flexible C-terminal tail. This is a composite interpretation of complementary evidence, not a single experimentally resolved full-length conformation. The main mature receptor excludes the cleaved signal peptide. The small resting inset focuses on the ectodomain, rather than showing an additional complete receptor.

EGF is represented at the I/III binding region of each receptor and the two extracellular domains interact through domain-II arms. The kinase interaction is labeled as activator C-lobe to receiver N-lobe. This represents an allosteric activation mechanism, not a requirement for activation-loop phosphorylation. The resting tethered state is one accessible conformation; the figure does not imply that all unliganded receptors are isolated tethered monomers. Higher oligomers, ligand-dependent asymmetry and other family heterodimers are outside this illustration.

The sequence strip follows canonical UniProt P00533 precursor coordinates:
- Signal peptide: 1–24, removed during maturation.
- Extracellular region: 25–645.
- Transmembrane helix: 646–668.
- Cytoplasmic region: 669–1210.
- UniProt protein-kinase annotation: 712–979.
The canonical precursor has 1,210 amino acids. These feature boundaries are annotation conventions; they are not a claim that every structural paper uses identical construct boundaries.

The pY symbols denote illustrative tail phosphotyrosines, not an exhaustive map or measured occupancy. No numbered phosphorylation sites are printed because historical mature-protein numbering differs by 24 residues from canonical precursor numbering. For example, conventional Y1068 corresponds to precursor Y1092; such conventions must not be mixed.

The pathway summary includes GRB2/SOS–RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK and GRB2/GAB1–PI3K–AKT. Intermediates and regulatory feedback are omitted. These arrows summarize signaling influence, not molecular conversion. The represented GAB1 recruitment route is via GRB2, not direct docking of GAB1 to a receptor phosphotyrosine. Receptor trafficking is indicated schematically without modeling sorting outcomes.

## Visual limitations retained after review
Secondary structures, interface shapes, glycan trees, flexible tails, helix packing and receptor poses are illustrative. They are not coordinates, bond-level depictions or measured distances. The kinase interface is stylized and should not be used for docking or structural interpretation. The left GRB2/GAB1 vignette remains visually crowded near the cyan tail despite targeted correction: use the explicit GRB2-to-GAB1 pathway and this note to interpret recruitment, not apparent contact between raster shapes. No atomic contact validation is claimed.

The spectrum, phosphoproteomic heatmap, density icon and conformational snapshots are illustrative, not an actual dataset. The density footer was corrected from an experimental-data label to “Illustrative density.” The drawing is not to scale, including its sequence blocks, receptor domains and ligand sizes.
