## Scientific framing

T4 is a multiprotein viral assembly. A protein capsid retains the genome outside the bacterium; receptor recognition and baseplate rearrangement are coupled to tail-sheath contraction and genome delivery. The illustration distinguishes outer membrane, peptidoglycan, inner membrane and bacterial cytoplasm. The host-recognition inset is a generic protein-receptor schematic, not an experimentally resolved interface. The envelope conduit, capsid geometry, genome packing and subunit stoichiometries are simplified. The illustration does not resolve all entry intermediates or molecular details. Genome delivery is represented by a single gold path. The proteomics footer represents complementary analyses; its spectrum, network and heatmap are illustrative data, not empirical results or a complete experimental workflow. This raster poster is not a validated atomic model or print-ready vector master.
