## Scientific limitations

Bovine rod rhodopsin is the named model, not microbial proton-pumping rhodopsin. The poster combines successive states, simplified chromophore geometry, a schematic G protein and conceptual electronic energy surfaces. It is not an atomic reconstruction or measured energy landscape. Early all-trans photoproduct formation and later Meta II activation are distinct. The protonated Schiff base in the dark state becomes deprotonated in Meta II; chemical charges are intentionally omitted in simplified geometry icons. CNG channels belong to plasma membrane, not the disc membrane containing the hero. Plots are illustrative. Quantum effects refer to absorption and excited-state photochemistry, not a claim of quantum computation or sustained coherence.
