## Scientific framing and limitations

The nuclear pore complex is a multiprotein assembly, schematically based on vertebrate architecture. Two lipid bilayers of the nuclear envelope join around the pore rim; the channel connects cytoplasm and nucleoplasm without opening into the perinuclear space. Flexible FG-nucleoporin domains contribute to selective permeability and interact with transport receptors. The illustrated import example does not imply all traffic is unidirectional. The diagram omits the full Ran-dependent transport cycle, species-specific stoichiometries, detailed luminal-ring architecture, and other transport pathways. Receptor and cargo structures, FG conformations, density map, spectrum and reconstructed pore icons are illustrative, not experimentally derived data. Protein-ring geometry, proportions and basket architecture are simplified. This is not an atomic-coordinate model or print-ready vector master.
