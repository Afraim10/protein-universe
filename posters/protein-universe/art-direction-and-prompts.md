## Art direction

Combine the Quasar poster's dominant composition, the Evolution Map's discoverable scientific vignettes, and the periodic tables' consistent visual vocabulary. Use a midnight indigo ground, cyan and violet molecular forms, coral folding-landscape terraces, and sparse amber binding-site accents. The main visual hierarchy is protein silhouette, folding and conformational motion, then proteomics.

## Scientific framing

The central protein is schematic. Ghost contours communicate alternative conformations. The energy landscape is an abstract projection, not a physical cellular location, and contains multiple routes and native substates. Functional disorder prevents an implication that all functional proteins require one compact fold. Proteoforms are molecular forms distinguished by sequence and modifications, not merely alternate conformations. The footer separates identity, abundance, proteoforms, and interactions; these require suitable, distinct measurements. The allostery and membrane scenes are conceptual and would need structure-specific refinement for an instructional publication.
