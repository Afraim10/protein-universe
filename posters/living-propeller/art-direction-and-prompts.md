## Scope and limitations

Simplified proton-driven Salmonella motor, not universal bacterial flagellar architecture. Whole-cell inset and molecular cutaway have different scales. Stator and rotor copy numbers, component geometry and filament proportions are illustrative. Assembly/export apparatus and chemotaxis signaling are omitted. Proton flow through peripheral inner-membrane stators is distinct from shaft rotation; ATP-driven export is not depicted as rotational fuel. No independent scientific validation or coordinate-derived rendering is claimed.
