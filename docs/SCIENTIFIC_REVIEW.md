# Scientific status and review

All included posters are AI-assisted schematic illustrations. The files preserve generation-time prompts and review notes; they have not undergone an independent full scientific audit for release.

Before educational reuse, inspect the specific claims against its cited structural resources and primary papers. Confirm protein identity, subunit count, topology, residue numbering, directionality, compartment labels and molecular formulas. Simplified atomic cartoons are not validated bond graphs.

Electrostatic potential is distinct from charge density and hydrophobicity. Property colors in the carbonic anhydrase poster are illustrative, not calculated. Rhodopsin energy surfaces are conceptual rather than measured. Main protein silhouettes are artistic approximations rather than exact PDB-derived surfaces.

For the launch set, read the notes next to EGFR, proteasome, rhodopsin and carbonic anhydrase. Earlier series references also retain their own limitations. Reference assets establish visual style, not scientific ground truth.

Report an issue with the poster title, exact label or region, suggested correction and an authoritative supporting reference. Confirmed fixes should receive a new version; preserve prior provenance and record what changed.
